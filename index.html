Michal Kulak

Head of Sustainable Investment Research at Robeco|IPBES Business and Biodiversity Expert

Michal Kulak leads Sustainable Investment Research at Robeco, integrating ESG factors into investment decisions.

He was a Coordinating Lead Author of the IPBES Business and Biodiversity Assessment (2023–2026), leading the chapter on measuring business impacts and dependencies on nature. He holds a doctorate from ETH Zurich, spent five years as a sustainability scientist at Unilever, and moved into asset management in 2019. He developed Robeco's Biodiversity Investment Framework and is a certifying expert for the Solar Impulse Foundation.
